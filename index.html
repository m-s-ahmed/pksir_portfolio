01 — About

Modeling the flow beneath complex systems — from magnetized fluids to leukemia therapy.

Dr. Md. Abu Bkar Pk is a Professor in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the University of Rajshahi, where his work spans two connected threads: the statistical theory of MHD (magneto-hydrodynamic) turbulence, and — more recently — fractional-order mathematical models applied to leukemia treatment, epidemic spread, and population dynamics.

Alongside teaching, he serves as Provost/Warden of the Shahid Mir Abdul Quayyum International Dormitory and as a guest instructor for the Departments of Electrical & Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, and Computer Science Engineering — carrying applied mathematics across the engineering curriculum.
